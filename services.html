Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Reach Orion.

Why should we choose a Headless CMS over traditional WordPress or Webflow?
Traditional CMS platforms bundle database queries, plugin dependencies, and frontend rendering into one fragile monolithic server. A headless architecture (like Next.js + Sanity) separates content storage from rendering. This results in sub-second load times globally, zero security plugin vulnerabilities, drastically reduced hosting costs, and complete freedom for both marketers and software engineers.
How long does a typical web architecture engagement take?
Standard B2B SaaS website rebuilds typically take 8 to 10 weeks from initial audit to production launch. We also offer express sprint tracks (4 to 6 weeks) for teams preparing for high-stakes venture capital funding announcements or product launches.
Can our marketing team edit and publish content without developers?
Yes, 100%. We configure our headless studios (such as Sanity Studio or Contentful) with intuitive visual page builders, real-time preview sidebars, and modular section libraries. Your marketers can launch new landing pages, change copy, adjust CTAs, and run campaigns in minutes without ever opening a code pull request.
What is your typical project investment range?
Our fixed-price projects typically range between $14,000 for focused headless migrations or component design systems, up to $38,000+ for comprehensive multi-region enterprise platforms. You can configure your exact requirements using our interactive project estimator.
